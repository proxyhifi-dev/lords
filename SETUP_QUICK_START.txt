🆘 EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If something goes wrong:

Option 1: FLATTEN via Dashboard
    1. Go to http://localhost:8000
    2. Click "FLATTEN POSITION" button
    3. All positions will be closed immediately at market prices

Option 2: STOP Bot + Manual Exit
    1. Press Ctrl+C to stop bot
    2. Log in to SAMCO app
    3. Go to "Square Off Positions"
    4. Select all positions
    5. Click "Square Off"

Option 3: Manual SAMCO Exit
    1. Log in to SAMCO app
    2. Go to "Active Positions"
    3. For each position, click "Exit" or "Square Off"
    4. Use market price (not limit)

CAPITAL=100000                     # Trading capital (₹)
